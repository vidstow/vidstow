Follow a playlist, then review what arrives later

Follow remembers a whole public playlist, along with the download settings you already picked on Home. Videos that are already there will not bug you as new. Checks happen only when you ask, and you personally choose which new videos head to the Queue.

The window below is a clickable simulation of the real thing. Step through it at your own pace, or try it in the app yourself — nothing here downloads or saves anything.

    Recommended changes

    The overall direction here is solid — I checked it against 4K Video Downloader+ running alongside, and the bones are right. What follows are seven fairly small changes that clear up the confusing spots without adding any new features. Each one shows what is there now and what I would ship instead.

    1

    Give Follow a little more weight on Home

    Right now the Follow button sits quietly under the playlist title, and most first-timers will blow straight past it on their way to Download. Its spot is exactly right — attached to the playlist itself rather than the current selection — it just needs a little hint underneath so people actually notice it is there.

    Now
    Building LLMs from scratch Playlist · 43 videos
    Recommended
    Building LLMs from scratch Playlist · 43 videos
    Saves future videos · Nothing downloads now

    Change: leave the button where it is, and add the hint line beneath it until someone follows for the first time.

    2

    Name the videos already here in the setup

    Saying “future videos only” is honest, but it leaves people guessing about the videos sitting right in front of them. If you are following a course halfway through, you have no way to tell whether those older lectures are quietly ignored or about to flood your review list.

    Now
    Follow this playlist? Future videos only. Videos already in this playlist will not appear as new. Manual checks. Open Following and choose Check now.
    Recommended
    Follow this playlist? Future videos only. The 43 already here won’t show up as new. Download all 43 now. Start those downloads, then keep following for new ones. Manual checks. We’ll only look for new videos when you hit Check now.

    Change: put the real number in the dialog, and let people pick — just the future videos, or download all 43 now.

    3

    Compress each Following row to three lines

    Four lines per row does not sound like much until you have three or four followed playlists with errors sprinkled in. At the smaller window size the whole page turns into a long scroll where everything shouts at the same volume and nothing stands out.

    Now
    Building LLMs from scratch Vizuara · 46 videos 3 to review · Last checked Just now Up to 1080p · File · English · SRT Couldn’t check for updates. Saved videos are still available.
    Recommended
    Building LLMs from scratch · Vizuara · 46 3 to review · Last checked Just now Check failed Up to 1080p · File · English · SRT Full folder path and error detail live in the detail view.

    Change: roll the channel and count up into the title line, keep one line for status and one for settings, and leave the full folder path for the detail view.

    4

    Show real check progress

    When the app just says “checking playlists,” you have no idea whether that means one playlist or five, or whether it will take seconds or minutes. 4K handles this nicely with a per-item spinner and a last-updated time, so even a slow run never feels stuck.

    Now
    Status bar: Checking playlists
    Row: Checking…
    Recommended
    Header: Checking 2 of 5 · Stop checking
    Row 2: Checking…Rows 3–5: Waiting to check

    Change: count it out in the header, like 2 of 5, and skip the percentage bar unless we genuinely know the total.

    5

    Guard the big review

    Pre-selecting three new lectures is a kindness. Pre-selecting forty is a trap, because one stray click on Download queues the whole lot — and the All available escape hatch sits quietly up above where nobody looks.

    Now
    40 of 40 selected
    Recommended
    40 new · showing 10 · 10 of 10 selected
    Shown videos only
    Confirm before queueing a batch above the page size.

    Change: page through large reviews ten or so at a time, keep Download and Skip scoped to what is on screen, and ask for confirmation before a big batch heads to the queue.

    6

    Reuse the same output editor when editing saved settings

    Home and Following both edit the exact same thing — quality plus File or Embed — but they look like completely different controls. Anyone comparing the two will reasonably conclude they are different features.

    Now
    Home: Video | 1080p 720p · File Embed · Title & channel Following edit: Video/Audio toggle + quality dropdown
    Recommended
    Up to 1080p · File · English · SRT · Edit One shared editor in both places. Saving notes that queued items keep the settings they started with.

    Change: build one shared editor and use it in both places, with the File and Embed wording kept identical.

    7

    Say what the two badges mean, once

    The sidebar shows two numbers that look like the same kind of backlog, but they mean totally different things: one counts videos still waiting for your decision, the other counts downloads already on their way. Nobody is going to guess that on their own.

    Now
    Following · 3Queue · 2
    Plus two messages for one skip: a floating banner and an inline notice.
    Recommended
    Queue 2 queued Following 40 to review Keep the compact number. Put the meaning under the label, not inside the pill.

    Change: leave the rail badge as a number. Add a quiet caption under Queue and Following so the two counts do not look like the same backlog. Spell it out once in the empty states, and merge the double skip messages into one.

    Put together with the 4K window open in the background for reference. Nothing here was downloaded or saved.
