7. Settings copy

The Settings board already has the rows p2 has: Show in Finder, Change…, confirm Off, Recheck, FFmpeg path, Engine ytdlp-go v0.3.0, privacy notice, Don't send, Clear history, Copy diagnostics, middot colophon. 8. p2 Downloads sizes are broken. Penpot is not.

p2 seeds history with sizeMB: POLICY['2160p'](26). That stores an object, so the list prints [object Object] MB. Penpot shows 1.7 GB, 220 MB, 101 MB, 242 MB, and 150 MB, which is what those calls were supposed to compute. Today rows 264 MB and 396 MB also match. Do not change the Penpot sizes to match the bug. Fix p2 if you want the prototype to display them.

Not a Penpot miss. The boards already have the intended numbers.
