Warm paper on main, to the new p2

Orange is origin/main Green is the proposed change

Latest main is still the cream app in the README. A 160px brown rail, Inter, an Aqua title bar, and no status bar. I am comparing that shipped window to the zinc p2 I would build next, not to p2.html.

Orange is a real README shot where one exists, or a mock drawn from Home.svelte on origin/main. Green is the same dock language as home-p2.html, then Queue, Downloads, and Settings in that chrome.

  • One zinc window
  • One paste field
  • Compact dock
  • 36px queue rows
  • Settings colophon
  • Live status bar

Already on main

Analyze still happens before anything enters the queue. Backend plans, capabilities, and tokens still author the choices. Playlist range, search, and the 500 cap stay. Batch lines still name ready, duplicate, invalid, and analysis failed.

Change… stays on every save bar. Recovery-required still replaces the workstation. The video-and-playlist modal stays.

Still to change

The cream canvas, the Single URL / Batch URLs switch, and page titles like Download from YouTube go. Queue cards at 116px go. About leaves the nav. Confirm before starting downloads is not rebuilt.

There is no status bar on main. The Aqua title bar sits on a cream window above a brown rail. That split is the shell I am replacing.

I would ship one zinc material. Canvas, sidebar, and status bar share the same surface. Path on the left of the status bar. Idle or live slots on the right. Home drops the page title. About leaves the nav. FFmpeg leaves the rail and lives under Engine on Settings.

This is not a light and dark theme. I am replacing the shipped shell.

What must survive the restyle

Paper to zinc is a lot of paint. None of it rewrites admission.

  • Analyze before anything enters the queue.
  • Backend-authored plans, capabilities, and tokens.
  • Change… on every save bar.
  • Batch line status, the 2 to 20 cap, and the 500-entry playlist cap.
  • The video-and-playlist modal, plus Review the playlist instead.
  • Subtitle, extra .srt, and title and channel extras.
  • Artwork and chapters always on for video.
  • FFmpeg gates on complete video and MP3.
  • Recovery-required replaces the workstation. No sidebar. No queue mutation.
  • Interrupted jobs restore as paused. Nothing auto-starts.
