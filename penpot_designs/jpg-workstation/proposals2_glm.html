Round 3: the full pass.

Prototype 2 now implements both review documents — the transaction Home, the admit handoff, the honest format model, the merged Settings. This page is an audit of that prototype: every view, every state, every transition, plus a code-level trace of the edge cases. Fifteen findings in three tiers. Since the audit: playlists were redesigned to enqueue and start directly from Home — episode checkboxes live on the dock, the Queue collection review and its waiting state were removed, and Pause/Resume collection was added. This resolves A2, A3, B1, B4, and C3 in the prototype; ten findings remain. Tier A breaks a flow. Tier B contradicts a rule the app set for itself. Tier C is polish. Every finding ends with the exact change, not a direction.

Mocks use the same mini-chrome as rounds one and two. The audited prototype is live — every finding below is reproducible there.

A · Breaks

Each of these stops a working flow. They require fixes, not decisions.

A1 · The confirm window eats your typing

After Add to Queue, the dock collapses to a confirmation line and a three-second timer resets Home. The field’s input handler only watches the result and error phases — so text typed during the confirm window is left alone until the timer fires, and the reset clears the field. Because the core loop is paste → Enter → Enter, users will typically start typing the next URL during this window. The reset deletes that text.

B3 · Retry bypasses the slot limit

Retry sets the job straight to downloading — with two slots busy, a retried job makes three concurrent transfers and the status bar reads 3/2 slots. The limit is a promise; retry quietly breaks it.

Exact change

Retry sets the job to waiting with a retrying · slot tail; the next tick promotes it within the limit. Instant feedback, honest accounting.

B4 · Video ceilings in an audio review

Switch a batch to Audio and open Review: the rows still read “up to 1080p”. Minor, but inconsistent with the purpose of the surface — reporting what each link can produce.

Exact change

When payload.mode === 'audio', review rows show “audio · M4A / Opus” instead of the video ceiling.

C · Polish

Small gaps. Each is a one-line to one-afternoon change; together they determine perceived quality.

C1 · The waiting job doesn’t explain itself

A waiting job shows “Speed 0.0 MB/s · ETA —”. The relevant information — slot occupancy — is available but not shown.

Change: in the inspector, waiting jobs get one line — “Waiting for a free slot · 2/2 busy — starts when one frees.”

C2 · Esc has no gradient

Esc during analyzing does nothing; with Details or Review expanded, it destroys the whole result instead of collapsing the expansion first.

Change: Esc collapses an open expansion first, cancels an in-flight analysis second, resets the slot last.

C3 · No select-all for 24 episodes

Inverting a selection in the collection review means twenty-four clicks. The real app has range selection; the mock has nothing.

Change: “All / None” beside the count in the review inspector. Decision for you.

C4 · Toasts can’t be dismissed

Non-completion toasts time out in three seconds with no way out, and no × anywhere.

Change: an × on every toast; click-away optional.

C5 · The Downloads badge grows forever

Eleven in one session; two hundred in a real one. A badge that is always large becomes noise and is ignored — the failure mode round 1 §4 identified.

Change: count only files added since the last visit to Downloads, clearing on view — or drop the badge and let the toast carry completion. Decision for you.

C6 · The focus ring doesn’t travel

Rows are focusable, but arrow keys don’t move between them. Round 2 §3 promised “arrow keys move the ring; Enter acts on it.”

Change: ↑/↓ (and j/k) move focus through the list; Enter selects; Space stays pause/resume.

C7 · Accessibility debt

Episode checkboxes are divs; format segments lack aria-pressed; toasts and the status bar aren’t aria-live; tooltips appear on hover only, never on keyboard focus.

Change: real inputs or role="checkbox" with tabindex; aria-pressed on segments; aria-live="polite" on toasts and the status bar; tooltips on :focus-within.

C8 · The placeholder undersells the field

“Paste a YouTube URL” — the same field takes playlists and a handful of links. The placeholder is the natural place to communicate the intake model.

Change: “Paste a YouTube link — or a few.”

D · Verified

Nine checks that passed

Checks that passed during the audit:

  • The field never moves, across all six Home states.
  • Esc, type-over, and the Enter-Enter loop behave as round 2 §11 specifies.
  • No fake format heights anywhere — a 720p-max video never offers 4K.
  • Playlist exceptions are named with sizes; totals are computed from the actual policy and selection.
  • Failure → Retry → success, with copyable mono detail.
  • Completion eviction → toast → badge, and the file lands in Downloads grouped under Today.
  • Merged Settings: live slot stepper, the above-4 warning, a working diagnostics section.
  • Downloads search and its empty-match state.
  • prefers-reduced-motion honored; contrast at or above the round 2 §8 floor.
